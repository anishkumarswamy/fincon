01

At a glance

This starter website does not use advertising trackers, analytics tools or a website form-submission service. It does not create an account or ask you to upload financial documents.

The enquiry form prepares a message in your own email application. It does not send anything by itself or save the information you enter on this site. You choose whether to send the draft and to which email address.

✳

Please don’t include account numbers, identity documents, passwords or other sensitive financial information in an initial enquiry.

02

Information and contact

If you prepare an enquiry, the name, email address and topic you enter are used in a draft on your device. This page does not transmit or store that draft. If you send it, your email provider and the recipient will process it under their own privacy practices.

Once the business receives a message, the responsible business should provide a complete privacy notice explaining who controls that information, why it is used, how long it is retained, who it is shared with and how to make a privacy request. Those operational details must be added before launch; this template does not invent them.

03

Third-party services

This design loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Your browser contacts those providers to load the assets, and they may receive technical information such as your IP address. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests.

No analytics, advertising pixels, social media embeds or payment services are configured in this template. If you add any service, update this notice and obtain any consent required before enabling it.

04

Data protection

Data protection requirements can differ depending on where the business operates and where clients are located. A business serving people in the UAE and India should assess applicable privacy and data-protection rules—including, where relevant, UAE federal or free-zone rules and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection framework.

Before collecting client information, the responsible business should confirm its lawful basis, provide appropriate notices, limit access, secure information, set retention periods and arrange any necessary processor or cross-border transfer safeguards. A footer link alone does not establish compliance.

This page describes the starter website as configured and is not a legal opinion, a regulatory assurance or a substitute for advice tailored to the operating business.

05

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to fill in or send the enquiry draft. You may also configure your browser to block third-party fonts or images, though this can change how the site appears.

When you communicate with the business, its privacy contact should explain how to ask questions or make a request about your personal information. Add a verified contact route and applicable request process before this website is published.

06

Updates and contact

Changes to the website, its hosting or the services offered may change how information is handled. Review and update this notice whenever those details change.

Before launch: replace this starter notice with the operating business’s reviewed policy, legal identity, address, privacy contact details, retention practices and any configured form, analytics or hosting providers.
